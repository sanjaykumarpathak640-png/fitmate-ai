Realistic avatar data in this folder (mh.bin, mh.json, tex/*)

Source: MakeHuman base mesh, morph targets, proxies (hair, eyebrows, eyelashes, eyes,
shoes) and skin textures, as distributed in the "makehuman-data" npm package, created by
the MakeHuman team (Manuel Bastioni, Jonas Hauquier, Thomas Larsson and contributors).

Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal. In September 2020 the MakeHuman project relicensed its
bundled assets (base mesh, targets, proxies, skins) as CC0, whatever way they were
obtained; the older "AGPL3" strings inside the original files predate that change.
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/oldsite/faq/what_changed_regarding_the_license_in_2020.html
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/makehuman/faq/are_makehuman_files_free.html

Only assets made by the MakeHuman team are included. Community assets with other
licences were left out. FitMate converted the files (sparse targets, resized textures,
grey hair textures for tinting, whitened eye sclera) with tools/pack_mh.py.
